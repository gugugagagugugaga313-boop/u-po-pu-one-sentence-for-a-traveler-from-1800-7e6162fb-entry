Entry for the [One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800](https://make.fun/competitions/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The task

Write one sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern
internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 —
using only words and things a person from that year already knows.

## The sentence

> Think of one road joining every post office, library, and market square on earth to your front door, so you can send a letter, read a book, or speak with anyone in a breath.

173 characters, one sentence. The road is the one image: every post office,
library, and market square joined into a single road that reaches the
traveler's own front door, carrying letters, books, and voices both ways
in a breath.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no dependencies, no network) writes the sentence
to `/output/entry.txt`. The text is fixed, so two runs produce
byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
